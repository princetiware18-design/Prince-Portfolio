01 — The story behind the portfolio

From verifying visa documents
to querying datasets.

I'm a B.Sc. Computer Science graduate from the University of Mumbai who found a love for the part of the job where careful checking turns into clear answers.

My first real taste of working with structured information was during a five-month internship at Riya Travel & Tours (I) Pvt. Ltd. in J.B. Nagar, Mumbai. I worked as a Visa & Travel Consultant intern, processing 500+ visa applications — tracking each one in Excel, verifying documents, and following up with clients by call and email. Every error had a real consequence for someone's travel plans, so accuracy stopped being a buzzword and became a habit.

While finishing my B.Sc. Computer Science at the University of Mumbai (7.3 CGPA), I started pulling in the other direction: instead of tracking rows by hand, I wanted to query them, clean them and visualise them. That's how SQL (MySQL), Excel, Power BI, Python (Pandas) and Google Sheets became my daily tools — and how the five projects below came to life.

I'm at the beginning of my data career, and I'm honest about that. What I bring is the discipline of a role where details mattered, a genuine curiosity for what a dataset is hiding, and the habit of documenting my work so anyone can follow it.
